“A Drive to Learn: Dad, Son, and Mobile Fines”

Scenario 1

Jack: “Dad, look! I finally got my car. I can drive now!”

Dad: “That's great, Jack. But remember, driving is a responsibility, not just freedom.”

Jack (laughs): “Come on, Dad. Everyone uses their phones while driving. I won’t get caught.”

Dad: “That’s where you’re wrong, son. Let me show you something.”

Scenario 2

Dad: “See this graph? It shows how many mobile phone fines have been issued each year since 2008.”

Jack: “Wait, what are shown by those lines?”

Dad: “The blue line shows fines given by police and the orange line is camera‐detected fines.”

Jack (Astonished): “Whoa! So many fines!”

Scenario 3

Dad: “Yes, now let’s look at fines across different jurisdictions.”

Dad: “The darker the blue, the more the numbers—and Victoria is at the top. We live in the heart of Victoria.”

Scenario 4

Dad: “Here’s a chart of different types of fines per jurisdiction.”

Jack: “Speeding and mobile‐phone fines are through the roof. I wanted to race with my friends.”

Scenario 5

Dad: “Look at the pie chart, Jack. The number of fines in 2023—your age group (25–34) is the most frequent.”

Jack (Ashamed): “I understand now why my friends pay so much for fines.”

Scenario 6

Dad: “Most of the drivers getting caught are your age—25 to 34 years old—in Victoria and NSW.”

Dad: “Each box here shows the state and age group. Colour intensity represents fines—darker means more fines.”

Jack: “Our state, Victoria, even crossed New South Wales! I understand why you always warn me, Dad.”

Scenario 7

Jack: “I just thought… if no one’s watching, I wouldn’t get caught.”

Dad: “Technology is always watching. But it’s not just about fines… it’s about staying safe.”

Jack: “Thanks, Dad. Because of you and your visual statistics, I realized the danger of getting fines. The variety of charts and visuals helped me compare and judge the stats.”
